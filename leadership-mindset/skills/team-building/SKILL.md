# Team Building

Runs the smallest set of rituals that keep a small team aligned, and handles the hard conversations. Inherits `founder-core/references/principles.md` and pulls from `founder/23-values.md` when it exists.

**Owner role:** Founder-CEO
**Stage:** Stage-independent (always on)

## Step 0 — Constraints & upstream
- Read `founder/constraints.md`.
- Upstream: team size and roles, `founder/23-values.md`, current rituals, and the specific situation if there is one.

## Inputs
Team size and roles, values, current rituals, the specific situation (if any).

## Process
1. **Install the minimum rituals:** a weekly 30-min team sync (metrics, blockers, decisions); a weekly 1:1 per direct report (their agenda first); a monthly retro; quarterly goals tied to the stage gate.
2. **Define role clarity with the Ownership Map:** every outcome has exactly one owner.
3. **Feedback protocol:** specific behavior → impact → request, within 48 hours; in private for corrections, in public for wins.
4. **Performance issues:** clarify the expectation → check for an SOP/tooling gap → a 30-day plan with measurable outcomes → decide. Document each step.
5. **Co-founder issues:** a written agreement on roles, equity/vesting, decision rights, and exit terms; revisit every 6 months.
6. **Scale rituals only when they break,** never preemptively.

## Outputs
`founder/26-team-operating-system.md` + situation-specific plans.

## Utils
**Ritual templates and agendas.** **Ownership map.** **Feedback script** (behavior/impact/request). **30-day performance plan template.** **Co-founder agreement checklist.**

## Gate
`Every outcome in the Ownership Map has one owner; 1:1s happening ≥ 90% of weeks.` End with `Gate: PASS/OPEN — because …`.

## Anti-patterns
Meetings as status readouts. Avoiding the hard conversation. Consensus on everything.

## Evals
- **Should fire:** "How do I run good 1:1s?" / "My co-founder and I keep clashing." / "Someone on the team isn't working out."
- **Should NOT fire:** "Write the hiring scorecard." (→ hiring-playbook) / "Define our values." (→ company-values)
- **Golden run checklist:** minimum rituals installed; ownership map with one owner per outcome; feedback protocol; performance-issue steps; co-founder agreement checklist; scale-when-broken rule; `26-team-operating-system.md` created; gate line emitted.
