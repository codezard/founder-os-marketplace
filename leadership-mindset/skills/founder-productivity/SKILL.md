# Founder Productivity

Protects maker time for the one activity that matters this stage, and watches for burnout. Inherits `founder-core/references/principles.md` (A9 constraints) and `references/stage-map.md` (stage → metric).

**Owner role:** Founder (self)
**Stage:** Stage-independent (always on)

## Step 0 — Constraints & upstream
- Read `founder/constraints.md` first — hours available and energy pattern drive everything here.
- Upstream: current stage, this week's calendar, current commitments.

## Inputs
Current stage, hours available, energy pattern, current commitments, this week's calendar.

## Process
1. **Identify the One Metric That Matters** for the current stage (from the stage map) and the single activity that moves it.
2. **Build the Week Template:** Maker blocks (≥ 2 hours, protected, on the one activity), Manager blocks (sales calls, reviews batched), one capped Admin block, and a hard stop.
3. **For side-founders:** map hours to days honestly — a 15-hour week is 2 weekday evenings + one weekend day, not "every evening".
4. **Daily:** three tasks max; the first one before any inbox.
5. **Weekly Review (30 min):** did the metric move? what to stop? next week's one activity? energy 1–5? Log it.
6. **Burnout tripwires:** two weeks of energy ≤ 2 → cut scope, not sleep.

## Outputs
`founder/week-template.md` + weekly review entries in `founder/weekly-reviews.md` (uses `founder-core/assets/templates/weekly-review.md`).

## Utils
**Week template.** **Daily card** (three tasks). **Weekly review questions.** **Energy tracker.** **Stage → one-activity mapping.**

## Gate
`≥ 60% of available hours spent on the one activity for 3 weeks.` End with `Gate: PASS/OPEN — because …`.

## Anti-patterns
Productivity systems as procrastination. Scheduling more than the hours that exist. Optimizing tools instead of doing the work.

## Evals
- **Should fire:** "I'm overwhelmed and can't focus." / "I have 15 hours a week — what do I do with them?" / "Plan my week."
- **Should NOT fire:** "Help me decide whether to pivot." (→ decision-frameworks) / "How do I run my team?" (→ team-building)
- **Golden run checklist:** constraints read; one-metric + one-activity identified for the stage; week template with protected maker blocks + hard stop; hours mapped to real days; daily three-task rule; weekly review ritual; burnout tripwire; `week-template.md` + `weekly-reviews.md` created; gate line emitted.
