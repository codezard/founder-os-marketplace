# Minimalist Review

Deletes complexity on a schedule, because it compounds faster than revenue. Inherits `founder-core/references/principles.md` (A7 minimalism).

**Owner role:** Founder-CEO
**Stage:** 4+ (quarterly)

## Step 0 — Constraints & upstream
- Read `founder/constraints.md`.
- Upstream: feature list with usage data, tool/subscription list with cost, channel list with CAC, meeting calendar, `founder/17-process-inventory.md`, headcount and roles. Gather any that are missing.

## Inputs
Feature/usage, tools/cost, channels/CAC, meetings, process inventory, headcount/roles.

## Process
1. **Build the Everything Inventory** across six categories: features, tools/subscriptions, channels, meetings, processes, commitments.
2. **Score each** on Contribution (revenue, retention, or time saved — with a number) vs. Cost (money + hours + complexity).
3. **Sort into Keep / Shrink / Kill / Merge.** Default is Kill unless evidence argues otherwise.
4. **For each Kill:** a sunset plan, customer communication if needed, and what to do with the freed hours.
5. **Compute the Complexity Dividend** — hours and money freed — and assign it explicitly to the one thing that matters this quarter.
6. **Write the "we stopped doing" list**, as visible as the roadmap.

## Outputs
`founder/24-minimalist-review-<quarter>.md`.

## Utils
**Inventory template** (six categories). **Keep/Shrink/Kill/Merge rubric.** **Sunset checklist.** **Complexity-dividend calculator.**

## Gate
`≥ 10% of tools, features, or meetings removed per quarter until the founder says there's nothing left.` End with `Gate: PASS/OPEN — because …`.

## Anti-patterns
Keeping things because they were hard to build. Cutting the one channel that works to "diversify". Reviews that produce a list and no deletions.

## Evals
- **Should fire:** "What should we cut this quarter?" / "We're doing too much — help me focus." / "Audit our tools and subscriptions."
- **Should NOT fire:** "How fast should we grow?" (→ sustainable-growth) / "Help me decide between two options." (→ decision-frameworks)
- **Golden run checklist:** six-category inventory; contribution-vs-cost scored with numbers; Keep/Shrink/Kill/Merge with Kill-default; sunset plans; complexity dividend assigned; "we stopped doing" list; ≥10% removed; `24-minimalist-review-<quarter>.md` created; gate line emitted.
