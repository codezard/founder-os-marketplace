# First Customers

Wins the first ten paying customers through warm-first acquisition and hands-on onboarding. Inherits `founder-core/references/principles.md` (A5 do it by hand before automating).

**Owner role:** Founder-Seller
**Stage:** 2 — MVP

## Step 0 — Constraints & upstream
- Read `founder/constraints.md`.
- Upstream: `founder/08-offer.md`, `founder/09-pricing.md` (v0), `founder/01-community-map.md`, `founder/outreach-tracker.csv`, and MVP readiness (`05-mvp-spec.md`). If the offer/pricing are missing, run those skills first.

## Inputs
Offer, pricing v0, community map, outreach tracker, MVP readiness.

## Process
1. **Define the Founding Customer deal:** reduced price locked 12 months + direct founder access, in exchange for a weekly feedback call, a testimonial, and a referral ask.
2. **Rank acquisition paths by speed:** warm network → community lighthouses → validation interviewees → cold outreach → content. Work them in that order.
3. **Onboard each customer by hand** (white-glove); record every question they ask — these become SOPs and FAQ.
4. **Run the 10-customer scoreboard:** name, source, date paid, date activated, time-to-value, disappointed-score, referral given.
5. **After every 5 customers, run a pattern review:** what's common in the ones who activated fastest? Tighten the ICP.
6. **Ask for the referral at the moment of first value**, with a written intro template they can forward.

## Outputs
`founder/11-first-customers.md` — the scoreboard and the pattern review.

## Utils
**Founding-customer agreement template** (price lock, access, obligations). **Onboarding checklist.** **Referral intro template** (forwardable). **Pattern-review questions:** fastest-activating cohort — same source? same trigger? same use case?

## Gate
`10 paying customers, each with ≥ 3 activated uses.` End with `Gate: PASS/OPEN — because …`. Recommend `pmf-measure` next.

## Anti-patterns
Free pilots without a written conversion condition. Discounting past the founding-customer terms. Onboarding via docs before onboarding by hand.

## Evals
- **Should fire:** "How do I get my first 10 customers?" / "I'm ready to launch — who do I sell to first?" / "Get me founding customers."
- **Should NOT fire:** "Do we have PMF?" (→ pmf-measure) / "Write cold emails." (→ cold-outreach)
- **Golden run checklist:** upstream read; founding-customer deal defined; paths ranked warm-first; white-glove onboarding + question capture; scoreboard; pattern review every 5; referral-at-first-value; `11-first-customers.md` created; gate line emitted.
