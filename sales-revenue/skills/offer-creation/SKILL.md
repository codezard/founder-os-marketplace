# Offer Creation

Turns the validated pain into an offer a stranger in the persona can repeat back. Inherits `founder-core/references/principles.md` (A3 sell before build) and `references/writing-standards.md`.

**Owner role:** Founder-Seller
**Stage:** 1 — Validate

## Step 0 — Constraints & upstream
- Read `founder/constraints.md` (delivery capacity, cash position for guarantees).
- Upstream: `founder/02-validation-report.md` (dream outcome, what they pay today). If absent, run `validate-idea` first.

## Inputs
Persona, dream outcome (from interviews), what they currently pay, delivery constraints.

## Process
1. **Define the Dream Outcome** in one sentence, in the customer's words.
2. **List every obstacle** between them and it; for each, name the solution component you will include.
3. **Increase perceived value** on three axes: speed (how fast to outcome), certainty (guarantee, proof), ease (done-for-you vs. tool).
4. **Name the offer around the outcome, not the mechanism.**
5. **Attach a guarantee** you can honor that removes the buyer's biggest risk (menu below).
6. **Add scarcity/urgency only if real** (cohort size, founder hours, launch price ending).
7. **Write the one-paragraph offer and a 30-second verbal pitch.**

## Outputs
`founder/08-offer.md` — dream outcome, components, value levers, name, guarantee, (real) scarcity, one-paragraph offer, 30-second pitch.

## Utils
**Value Equation:** perceived value = (Dream Outcome × Likelihood of achievement) ÷ (Time delay × Effort/sacrifice). Pull two levers up, two down.
**Guarantee menu:** unconditional refund / conditional ("do X, if no result, refund") / performance ("pay when you hit the outcome") / service ("we keep working free until…"). Pick the strongest the cash position survives.
**Naming formulas:** outcome + timeframe ("Audit-Ready in 30 Days"); persona + outcome; "The <Job> System".

## Gate
`A stranger in the persona can restate the offer's value in one sentence.` End with `Gate: PASS/OPEN — because …`. Recommend `pricing-strategy` and `landing-page` next.

## Anti-patterns
Fake scarcity. Guarantees the cash position can't survive. Naming the technology instead of the result.

## Evals
- **Should fire:** "What should I actually sell?" / "How do I make this offer irresistible?" / "Help me productize my consulting."
- **Should NOT fire:** "How much should it cost?" (→ pricing-strategy) / "Write the landing page." (→ landing-page)
- **Golden run checklist:** upstream read; dream outcome in customer words; obstacle→component map; 3 value levers; outcome-based name; honorable guarantee; real-or-omitted scarcity; one-paragraph + 30-sec pitch; `08-offer.md` created; gate line emitted.
