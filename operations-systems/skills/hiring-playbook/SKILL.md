# Hiring Playbook

Hires against measurable outcomes, not vibes, and only when the work justifies it. Inherits `founder-core/references/principles.md` (A5) and pulls values from `founder/23-values.md` when it exists.

**Owner role:** Founder-CEO
**Stage:** 4 — Processize

## Step 0 — Constraints & upstream
- Read `founder/constraints.md` (cash position, budget, location/legal).
- Upstream: `founder/17-process-inventory.md` and the role's SOP coverage. If the role has no SOP, refuse a full hire and route to `sop-builder` / contractor / `automation-workflows`.

## Inputs
Process inventory, revenue and cash position, the role's SOP coverage, budget, location/legal constraints.

## Process
1. **Hire-or-not check:** is the work ≥ 20 hours/week, documented, and paying for itself within 6 months? If not → contractor or automation.
2. **Write the Scorecard, not the job description:** mission (one sentence), 3–5 measurable 12-month outcomes, 5 competencies with behavioral definitions.
3. **Source:** network first, communities second, job boards last. Draft outreach via `cold-outreach` in partnership mode.
4. **Interview loop:** 20-min screen (motivation, outcomes) → work sample (real task from the SOP, paid if > 2 hours) → structured interview (one competency per question, past-behavior only) → two reference calls (with "would you rehire?").
5. **Score every candidate on the same rubric before discussing.** Decide within 48 hours.
6. **Onboarding plan:** 30/60/90 outcomes, SOP reading list, first delegated task from the delegation plan, weekly 1:1 agenda.

## Outputs
`founder/hiring/<role>-scorecard.md`, an interview kit, and an onboarding plan (uses `founder-core/assets/templates/scorecard.md`).

## Utils
**Scorecard template.** **Work-sample bank by role.** **Structured-interview question bank.** **Reference-call script.** **Remote work-sample integrity notes.** **30/60/90 template.**

## Gate
`New hire owns their outcomes by day 90 without founder involvement in daily execution.` End with `Gate: PASS/OPEN — because …`.

## Anti-patterns
Hiring for potential when you need output. Hiring a friend without the same loop. Unstructured "culture fit" interviews.

## Evals
- **Should fire:** "Should I hire my first employee?" / "Write a job scorecard and interview questions." / "Contractor or full-time?"
- **Should NOT fire:** "Delegate this to my VA." (→ delegation-framework) / "Define our values." (→ company-values)
- **Golden run checklist:** SOP coverage confirmed (or routed away); hire/no-hire check; scorecard with measurable outcomes + competencies; sourcing order; structured loop with paid work sample; same-rubric scoring; 30/60/90; `hiring/<role>-scorecard.md` created; gate line emitted.
