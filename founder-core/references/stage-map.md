# Founder OS — Stage Map to the First $1M

Six stages. Each has an objective, the skills that run in it, the roles active, the metric that matters, and a **gate** you must pass to exit. Do not run stage N+1 skills while stage N's gate is open. Run `/founder-os:status` (the `gate_check.py` script) at any time to see which gates are open or closed.

## Stage 0 — Discover (weeks 0–2)

- **Objective:** Produce a shortlist of 3–5 experience-gap problems with an identified community where the sufferers already gather.
- **Skills:** 1 find-business-idea, 2 community-finder
- **Roles active:** Founder (researcher hat)
- **Metric:** Number of gaps scoring ≥ 7/10 on the Gap Scorecard
- **Gate:** At least one gap with a named community of ≥ 500 reachable people and a founder-advantage score ≥ 7.

## Stage 1 — Validate (weeks 2–6)

- **Objective:** Kill or confirm the top gap using money, time, or access evidence.
- **Skills:** 3 validate-idea, 4 market-research, 5 competitive-analysis, 9 offer-creation (v0), 8 landing-page (v0)
- **Roles active:** Founder (researcher + seller hats)
- **Metric:** Validation evidence type collected; conversion of landing page to pre-order/waitlist-with-deposit
- **Gate:** ≥ 3 people paid or committed money, OR ≥ 5 people using a manual version weekly. Anything less → back to Stage 0 with the next gap.

## Stage 2 — MVP (weeks 6–14)

- **Objective:** Deliver the promised outcome to the first customers, by hand if necessary.
- **Skills:** 6 mvp-scoper, 8 landing-page (v1), 10 pricing-strategy (v0), 12 first-customers
- **Roles active:** Founder (builder + seller), possibly one contractor
- **Metric:** Time-to-first-value for a new customer; % of MVP customers who would be upset if it disappeared
- **Gate:** 10 paying customers who have used it ≥ 3 times, and ≥ 40% say "very disappointed" if it vanished.

## Stage 3 — First Customers → Repeatable Sale (months 3–8)

- **Objective:** Find one channel and one message that acquires customers predictably.
- **Skills:** 11 cold-outreach, 13 objection-handling, 14 marketing-plan, 15 copywriting, 7 pmf-measure, 25 unit-economics
- **Roles active:** Founder (seller + marketer), first part-time hire or VA
- **Metric:** CAC by channel, payback period, month-2 retention
- **Gate:** One channel producing ≥ 10 new customers/month at CAC payback < 6 months. MRR ≥ $8–10k.

## Stage 4 — Processize (months 6–14)

- **Objective:** Remove the founder from delivery and from routine acquisition.
- **Skills:** 20 processize, 21 sop-builder, 22 automation-workflows, 23 delegation-framework, 24 hiring-playbook, 16 seo-content, 17 email-campaigns
- **Roles active:** Founder (operator), 1–3 hires/contractors
- **Metric:** Founder hours in delivery per week (target → 0); SOP coverage % of recurring tasks
- **Gate:** Business runs for two weeks with the founder doing ≤ 5 hours of operational work. MRR ≥ $25–30k.

## Stage 5 — Grow Sustainably to $1M ARR (months 12–30)

- **Objective:** Add a second channel, expand revenue per customer, protect margin and culture.
- **Skills:** 18 social-media, 19 paid-ads, 10 pricing-strategy (v2), 26 financial-model, 27 pitch-deck / 28 fundraising (only if required), 29 company-values, 31 sustainable-growth, 30 minimalist-review, 32–34 leadership set
- **Roles active:** Founder (CEO), small team (3–8)
- **Metric:** Net revenue retention, gross margin, founder time on strategy vs. operations
- **Gate:** $83k MRR sustained for 3 months with gross margin ≥ 60% and NRR ≥ 100%. That is the $1M.

## Stage-independent (always on)

- 32 decision-frameworks, 33 founder-productivity, 34 team-building — run whenever a decision, a slump, or a hire comes up.
