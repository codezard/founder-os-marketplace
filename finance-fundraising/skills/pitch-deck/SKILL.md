# Pitch Deck

Builds the deck from a narrative spine, only for businesses that should raise. Inherits `founder-core/references/writing-standards.md` and pulls proof from traction and unit economics.

**Owner role:** Founder-CEO
**Stage:** 5 — Grow Sustainably

## Step 0 — Constraints & upstream
- Read `founder/constraints.md`.
- Upstream: `founder/22-fundraising-decision.md` = **Raise**. If the decision is Bootstrap or undecided, refuse and route to `fundraising` first. Also read positioning, market brief, traction, unit economics, and the financial model.

## Inputs
Positioning, market brief, traction data, unit economics, financial model, team, the ask.

## Process
1. **Confirm the fundraising verdict is Raise;** otherwise route to `fundraising`.
2. **Write the Narrative Spine first** (12 sentences, one per slide) before any slide: Problem → Why now → Solution → Traction → Business model → Market (bottom-up) → Competition & moat → Go-to-market → Team (why you) → Financials → Ask & use of funds → Vision.
3. **Every slide:** one headline that is a claim, one proof element, ≤ 30 words.
4. **Build the appendix:** cohort data, unit economics, detailed model, customer quotes.
5. **Produce the deck** (pptx) plus a 3-minute verbal version and a 30-second version.
6. **Objection prep:** the 15 questions investors will ask, with data-backed answers.

## Outputs
`founder/21-pitch-deck.pptx` + `founder/21-pitch-narrative.md` + an investor Q&A.

## Utils
**Narrative spine template** (12 slides). **Slide headline formulas** (claim + one proof). **Investor-question bank.** **Design constraints:** one idea per slide, consistent proof format.

## Gate
`A non-expert restates the business in one sentence after slide 3.` End with `Gate: PASS/OPEN — because …`.

## Anti-patterns
Top-down TAM. Feature slides. Team slide before traction when traction exists. Decks for businesses that should bootstrap.

## Evals
- **Should fire:** "Make my seed pitch deck." / "Slides for investors." / (fundraising returned Raise)
- **Should NOT fire:** "Should I even raise?" (→ fundraising) / "Build my financial model." (→ financial-model)
- **Golden run checklist:** Raise verdict confirmed (or routed); 12-sentence spine first; one-claim slides ≤30 words; appendix; 3-min + 30-sec versions; 15-question objection prep; `21-pitch-deck.pptx` + narrative created; gate line emitted.
