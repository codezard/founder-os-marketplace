# SEO Content

Turns the customer's jobs-to-be-done into a content plan built for both classic search and AI-answer engines. Inherits `founder-core/references/writing-standards.md`.

**Owner role:** Founder-Marketer / Content lead
**Stage:** 4 — Processize

## Step 0 — Constraints & upstream
- Read `founder/constraints.md` (hours/week for content sets the cadence).
- Upstream: `founder/12-marketing-plan.md` selecting content as primary/secondary. If content wasn't chosen, confirm before proceeding.

## Inputs
ICP, product, competitors, existing domain authority, hours/week for content.

## Process
1. **Build the Topic Map** from the customer's jobs: "how to [job]", "[job] template", "[competitor] alternative", "[regulation/portal] explained", "[job] mistakes".
2. **Prioritize by intent** (transactional > commercial > informational) and by achievable difficulty for the current domain.
3. **Design 3–5 Content Pillars**, each with a cluster of 6–10 supporting pieces.
4. **Per piece:** outline with search intent stated, a unique angle from founder experience, one proprietary asset (template, calculator, data), internal links, and a CTA matched to awareness level.
5. **Write for both classic search and AI answers:** direct answer in the first 100 words, structured headers, cited data, an FAQ block.
6. **Set a sustainable cadence** for the stated hours and a 90-day review (impressions, clicks, conversions per piece).

## Outputs
`founder/13-content-plan.md` + per-article briefs in `founder/content/`.

## Utils
**Topic-map template.** **Intent classifier** (transactional/commercial/informational). **Article-brief template** (intent, angle, proprietary asset, internal links, CTA). **On-page checklist.** **Programmatic-SEO pattern guide** for when a dataset can seed hundreds of pages.

## Gate
`≥ 3 pieces on page 1 for commercial-intent terms within 6 months, OR ≥ 1 signup/week from organic.` End with `Gate: PASS/OPEN — because …`.

## Anti-patterns
Thin AI-generated articles with no proprietary insight. Chasing volume keywords the ICP doesn't search. Publishing without CTAs.

## Evals
- **Should fire:** "Plan my SEO content." / "What should I blog about to rank?" / "Set up a programmatic SEO play."
- **Should NOT fire:** "Write one landing page." (→ landing-page) / "Draft my newsletter." (→ email-campaigns)
- **Golden run checklist:** cadence fit to hours; topic map from jobs; intent-prioritized; 3–5 pillars + clusters; per-article briefs with proprietary asset; AI-answer formatting; `13-content-plan.md` + `content/` created; gate line emitted.
