# Marketing Plan

Chooses the fewest channels that can work and commits to a measurable 90-day test. Inherits `founder-core/references/principles.md` (A7 minimalism) and the glossary (CAC, payback).

**Owner role:** Founder-Marketer
**Stage:** 3 — First Customers → Repeatable Sale

## Step 0 — Constraints & upstream
- Read `founder/constraints.md` (hours, budget, existing assets).
- Upstream: `founder/06-pmf-report.md` (at least Emerging) and `founder/01-community-map.md`. If PMF is Absent, refuse and route back to `pmf-measure`/`mvp-scoper`.

## Inputs
ICP, positioning, PMF report, budget, hours/week, existing assets (audience, content, network).

## Process
1. **Map attention to channels.** From the community map, map where the ICP already pays attention to the 8 channel families: outbound, content/SEO, community, partnerships, paid, social, referral, marketplaces/integrations.
2. **Score each channel** on Reach × Fit × Founder-skill × Cost × Time-to-signal. Pick **one primary and one secondary**. Explicitly reject the rest for this quarter.
3. **Write the Message Hierarchy:** one core promise, three supporting proofs, five customer phrases to reuse.
4. **Build the 90-day plan:** weekly actions, owner, leading metric per channel, and kill/scale thresholds at day 45 and day 90.
5. **Define the measurement stack:** UTMs, one dashboard, a weekly review ritual.

## Outputs
`founder/12-marketing-plan.md` — channel scores, primary/secondary pick, message hierarchy, 90-day plan, thresholds, measurement stack.

## Utils
**Channel scoring matrix** (1–5 each): Reach, Fit, Founder-skill, Cost (inverted), Time-to-signal (inverted).
**Message hierarchy template.** **Kill/scale thresholds** by channel (leading-metric floor at day 45).

## Gate
`Primary channel produces a leading-metric signal within 45 days.` End with `Gate: PASS/OPEN — because …`.

## Anti-patterns
Running 4+ channels before one works. Choosing channels the founder hates (they won't sustain them). Brand campaigns before direct response works.

## Evals
- **Should fire:** "How do I actually get customers?" / "What's my go-to-market?" / "Which channel should I focus on?"
- **Should NOT fire:** "Write the ad copy." (→ copywriting) / "Set up my email drip." (→ email-campaigns)
- **Golden run checklist:** PMF ≥ Emerging confirmed; 8 families mapped; scored; one primary + one secondary; others explicitly rejected; message hierarchy; 90-day plan with day-45/90 thresholds; `12-marketing-plan.md` created; gate line emitted.
