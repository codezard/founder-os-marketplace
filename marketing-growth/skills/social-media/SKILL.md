# Social Media

Grows attention on one platform where the ICP actually is, in the founder's voice. Inherits `founder-core/references/writing-standards.md`.

**Owner role:** Founder-Marketer (founder's own voice)
**Stage:** 5 — Grow Sustainably

## Step 0 — Constraints & upstream
- Read `founder/constraints.md` (hours/week and comfort level set the cadence).
- Upstream: ICP and the founder's story material. If no ICP is settled, pull from `pmf-measure`.

## Inputs
ICP, platform(s) where they are, the founder's story bank, hours/week, comfort level.

## Process
1. **Choose one platform** — where the ICP is, not where the founder is comfortable.
2. **Define 3 content pillars:** expertise (how-to from experience gaps), proof (customer stories, numbers), journey (build-in-public, decisions, lessons).
3. **Build a Story Bank** from the founder's career: 20 concrete moments, each with a number and a lesson.
4. **Set the cadence** (e.g. 3 posts + 10 meaningful comments/week) and a batching ritual.
5. **Draft a 2-week calendar** with hooks; each post ends with a soft path to the product or list.
6. **Monthly:** keep the top 20% of formats, drop the rest.

## Outputs
`founder/15-social-plan.md` + `founder/story-bank.md` (20 moments; read by copywriting and pitch-deck).

## Utils
**Pillar planner.** **Hook formulas.** **Comment-strategy guide.** **Story-bank interview questions.** **Humanizer checklist** (remove generic/AI tells; keep concrete specifics).

## Gate
`≥ 2 inbound conversations/month attributable to social.` End with `Gate: PASS/OPEN — because …`.

## Anti-patterns
Engagement bait. Posting on four platforms. Generic motivational content unconnected to the ICP's problems.

## Evals
- **Should fire:** "Help me build in public on LinkedIn." / "Plan my founder-brand content." / "What should I post on X?"
- **Should NOT fire:** "Write my nurture emails." (→ email-campaigns) / "Run ads for me." (→ paid-ads)
- **Golden run checklist:** one platform chosen (where ICP is); 3 pillars; 20-moment story bank; cadence fit to hours; 2-week calendar with hooks + soft CTA; monthly prune rule; `15-social-plan.md` + `story-bank.md` created; gate line emitted.
