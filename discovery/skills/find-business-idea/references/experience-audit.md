# Experience Audit — question bank & lenses

## The 25 prompts

Run these per past role, project, or side gig. The goal is concrete, repeated, costly work — not opinions.

### Repetition & friction
1. What did you do every week that felt manual or beneath your skill level?
2. What task did you dread on the calendar?
3. Where did you keep a personal spreadsheet or script to survive a bad tool?
4. What did you copy-paste between systems?
5. What broke often enough that you had a workaround memorized?

### Who else suffers
6. Who else in the org did the same painful task?
7. Who did this at every company in your industry?
8. Which vendor/partner role hated the same thing from their side?
9. Who paid the price when it went wrong — and how did that show up?

### Cost & stakes
10. What did a mistake here cost in money, time, or reputation?
11. What was the deadline or audit that made it urgent?
12. How many hours a month did the whole team lose to it?
13. What did you spend money on trying to fix it?

### Domain depth (the moat)
14. What took you > 2 years to learn that outsiders get wrong?
15. What regulation, format, or edge case do only insiders know?
16. What "obvious" advice is actually wrong in your field?
17. What data or access did you have that others can't easily get?

### Demand signals
18. What did people repeatedly ask you to help with or explain?
19. What did colleagues try to hire or bribe you to do?
20. What tools did people buy that still didn't solve it?
21. What did you almost build internally?

### Trigger events
22. What event kicks off this pain (new hire, new client, quarter close, launch, inspection)?
23. How predictable is that event?
24. Who feels it first, and who signs the check to make it stop?
25. If you could wave a wand, what one outcome would you hand them?

## "Boring is good" checklist

Durable, low-competition, high-willingness-to-pay problems cluster here:

- Compliance & regulatory filings
- Reporting & reconciliation
- Data cleanup / migration / de-duplication
- Scheduling & coordination
- Document handling (generation, review, routing, retention)
- Billing, invoicing, and collections
- Onboarding/offboarding checklists

If a gap is in one of these and scores high on founder advantage, weight it up.
